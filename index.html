The Monthly Rug Design Award

One award. Every month. Original rug and textile designs from makers around the world — celebrated, shared, and remembered.

❤

What it is

A rotating monthly spotlight for original carpet and rug designs — hand-knotted, woven, digital, or conceptual. Each month we feature one winning design on this page, across Hridaya social channels, and in the award archive with permanent provenance.

How to enter

  1. One design per month — original work only, no resubmissions of prior entries.
  2. Submit by the 20th — late entries roll into the next cycle automatically.
  3. Include your story — materials, origin, inspiration. Judges read it.
  4. Free to enter — no fees, ever. Winners are chosen by a rotating panel plus community favorites.

Submit your design

JPG, PNG, or PDF. Max 20 MB. High resolution preferred.
